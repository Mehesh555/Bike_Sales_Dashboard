#  Bike Sales Analytics Dashboard | SQL + Power BI

##  Project Overview

The **Bike Sales Analytics Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using **MySQL and Microsoft Power BI**. The dashboard analyzes bike sales performance, revenue trends, brand-wise performance, customer ratings, discounts, and bike categories.

The goal of this project is to transform raw bike sales data into meaningful business insights through SQL analysis, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations.

##  Project Objectives

- Analyze overall bike sales and revenue performance.
- Compare revenue across different bike brands.
- Monitor monthly revenue and units sold.
- Understand bike body style distribution.
- Track average selling price, discounts, and customer ratings.
- Analyze electric bike sales performance.
- Build an interactive dashboard for business decision-making.

##  Dashboard KPIs

The dashboard includes the following key performance indicators:

1. **Average Discount** – Tracks the average discount percentage.
2. **Total Revenue** – Displays overall revenue generated.
3. **Average Customer Rating** – Measures customer satisfaction.
4. **Total Units Sold** – Shows the total number of bikes sold.
5. **Average Selling Price** – Calculates the average selling price.
6. **Electric Units Sold** – Tracks electric bike sales.

##  Dashboard Visualizations

### 1. Monthly Revenue & Units Sold
- Analyzes monthly revenue trends.
- Compares revenue and units sold over time.
- Helps identify sales patterns.

### 2. Brand-wise Revenue Performance
- Compares revenue across different bike brands.
- Identifies top-performing brands.
- Highlights brand-level business performance.

### 3. Bike Body Style Distribution
- Displays the distribution of bikes by body style.
- Uses a donut chart to compare bike categories.
- Helps understand customer purchasing preferences.

### 4. Dynamic Bike Details
- Displays bike images based on the selected brand.
- Shows Engine CC, Mileage, Fuel Type, and Body Style.
- Displays an all-bikes overview when no specific brand is selected.

##  Interactive Features

- Dynamic brand selection using brand logos.
